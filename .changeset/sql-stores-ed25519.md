Ed25519 (`v1a`) signatures from the Standard Webhooks spec: `signing: "ed25519"` on `createVector` or per endpoint, `endpoints.publicKey()`, `generateKeyPair()`, `publicKeyFor()` and `vector keypair`. `verify` accepts `whpk_` public keys. New SQLite (`@sweberdev/vector/sqlite`) and MySQL (`@sweberdev/vector/mysql`) stores.
